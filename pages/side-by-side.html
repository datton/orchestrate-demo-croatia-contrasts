Side by side

The same coast, two very different ways to spend a week.

Istria

Landscape: green hills, vineyards, forest, hill towns.

Food: truffles, olive oil, pršut, Malvazija and Teran.

History: Roman, Venetian, Austrian, Italian; bilingual signs in places.

Pace: wandering from village to village.

Getting around: a car makes the inland towns easy.

Dalmatian islands

Landscape: white karst stone, scrub, lavender, open sea.

Food: fish, olive oil, island wine.

History: Greek and Venetian, with walled harbour towns.

Pace: slow, set by the sea and the ferries.

Getting around: ferries between islands, then local roads and boats.

What they share

Both are on the Adriatic, both have stone old towns, olive oil and good wine, and both have a Venetian chapter in their history. The difference is mostly one of ground and rhythm: green hills on one side, white stone and water on the other.
