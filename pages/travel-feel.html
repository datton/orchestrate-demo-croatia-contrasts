How each one feels

How each place feels through the year, and how to choose.

Seasons

  • Istria in autumn: the olive harvest and the truffle season, with cooler air for walking between hill towns.
  • Istria in spring and early summer: green countryside and mild days.
  • Islands in summer: long hot days and the sea at its warmest; lavender on Hvar blooms in early summer.
  • Islands in spring and autumn: quieter harbours and gentler heat.

The mood

Istria feels like a countryside holiday with a sea edge: slow lunches, vineyards, and small stone towns found at the end of winding roads. The islands feel like a holiday made of water: mornings by the harbour, afternoons swimming from rocks, evenings when the ferry horn is the loudest sound.

How to choose

  • Want food, wine and wandering? Start with Istria.
  • Want sea, stone and an unhurried pace? Try the islands.
  • Have two weeks? Consider both: they sit at opposite ends of the coast.
